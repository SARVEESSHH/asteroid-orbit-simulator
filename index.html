🧠 Scientific Conclusion

The simulation demonstrates how orbital parameters influence the size, shape, and period of celestial orbits. Earth occupies the inner Solar System, while Vesta and Ceres reside within the Main Asteroid Belt between Mars and Jupiter. Applying Kepler's Third Law shows that larger orbital distances correspond to longer orbital periods.
